Privacy policy

This policy explains how Estavra Health Ltd ("we", "us") handles personal information when you visit estavrahealth.com or contact us. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, which you can find in the app and on its store listing.

Who we are

Estavra Health Ltd is the data controller for this website. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 17583911, with our registered office at 128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX. You can contact us about privacy at privacy@estavrahealth.com.

What we collect

  • When you browse this website: we do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Our web host keeps standard technical logs, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site running securely. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.
  • When you email us: we receive your name, email address and anything you choose to include in your message.

How we use it

We use your information only to run this website and to reply to you. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in operating the site and answering enquiries. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the website and our email, who process it on our behalf. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep emails for as long as we reasonably need them, for example to deal with your enquiry and to keep our business records.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit its use. To do this, email privacy@estavrahealth.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.
