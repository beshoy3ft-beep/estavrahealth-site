SurgiGuide privacy policy

SurgiGuide is a patient information app provided by Estavra Health Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we handle when you use the app.

What we collect

You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, contact details or medical records.

If you use the Ask feature, we process the question you type or speak so we can answer it. Questions are handled anonymously and aren't linked to you or your device. Please don't include your name or other personal details in your questions.

We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics, and we never sell your information.

Why we use it

We use your questions to answer them and to improve the information in the app. Our lawful basis under UK data protection law is our legitimate interest in providing a safe and useful patient information service.

How long we keep it

Anonymised questions and answers are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted.

Who processes it

We use carefully selected service providers, such as a secure cloud hosting provider and an AI service provider, to run the app. They process information only on our instructions, under contracts that protect it, and it is encrypted in transit. Where a provider is based outside the UK, appropriate safeguards are in place.

Children

SurgiGuide is intended for adults (16 and over).

Not medical advice

SurgiGuide gives general information only. It doesn't diagnose, treat or assess symptoms, and it doesn't replace your surgical team, GP or NHS 111. In an emergency, call 999.

Your rights

You have rights under UK data protection law, including the right to ask what information we hold about you. Because questions are anonymous, we usually can't link them to you. To ask about your information, email privacy@estavrahealth.com. For anything else about SurgiGuide, email surgiguide@estavrahealth.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date below.
